Score a touchdown to unlock the next level. Level 1 is very easy; defenders become faster and coverage tighter each level. From Level 3 onward, the defense automatically adds a second pass rusher; choose whether to add a second blocker. Earn a first down to reset your four downs. Tip: Watch the cornerbacks and safety. A receiver with more separation is easier to complete a pass to. You get a final touchdown-only play if you fail to make a first down in four downs. Each play ends with a catch, incompletion, interception, or sack. A catch gains yards based on how far downfield the receiver is.

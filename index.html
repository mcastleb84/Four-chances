Score a touchdown to unlock the next level. Level 1 is very easy; defenders become faster and coverage tighter each level. From Level 3 onward, the defense automatically adds a second pass rusher; choose whether to add a second blocker. Earn a first down to reset your four downs. Tip: Watch the cornerbacks and safety. A receiver with more separation is easier to complete a pass to. After four downs without a first down, you get exactly one final touchdown-only play. Plays automatically reset and snap after a short pause; you can also tap SNAP BALL immediately. The offense begins on its own 20-yard line. Each completed pass moves the ball downfield; a touchdown only happens when the ball reaches the opponent’s end zone. Each play ends with a catch, incompletion, interception, or sack. A catch gains yards based on how far downfield the receiver is.
